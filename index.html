🌿 Plant Care Tips

Keep your green friends happy and healthy

💧

Watering

Check the soil before watering. Most indoor plants need water only when the top inch of soil is dry.

☀️

Sunlight

Most plants love indirect sunlight. Avoid placing them in direct harsh sun which can scorch the leaves.

🌡️

Temperature

Keep plants away from ACs and heaters. Room temperature of 18–28°C suits most houseplants.

🪣

Drainage

Always use pots with drainage holes to avoid root rot from waterlogging.

🌻

Fertilizing

Feed plants once a month during growing season (spring/summer) with liquid fertilizer.

✂️

Pruning

Remove dead or yellow leaves regularly. This encourages new healthy growth.
